# 🛠️ Networking Fundamentals — Projects

Welcome to the **Networking Fundamentals Projects**.

The lessons teach networking concepts.

The labs give you guided hands-on practice.

The projects ask you to **put those skills together and solve larger networking problems**.

Throughout the course, you'll complete **seven projects**. Each project appears after a major group of lessons and becomes progressively more independent.

By the final project, you'll be expected to design, configure, secure, document, test, and troubleshoot a small-business network.

---

# 🎯 Why Projects?

It's possible to memorize networking terminology without knowing what to do when something actually stops working.

These projects are designed to bridge that gap.

You'll practice skills such as:

- Network design
- IP addressing
- Subnetting
- Device configuration
- Switching
- Routing
- VLANs
- DHCP
- DNS
- NAT
- Wireless networking
- Network documentation
- Security
- Packet analysis
- Troubleshooting
- Technical decision-making

Projects also reinforce concepts that may appear on the **CompTIA Network+ N10-009** exam.

---

# 🎓 Network+ Connection

These projects are also designed to reinforce skills useful for the **CompTIA Network+ N10-009** exam.

Instead of only asking:

> What does DHCP do?

you may need to determine:

> Why did this workstation receive a `169.254.x.x` address, and what should you investigate?

Instead of only asking:

> What is a VLAN?

you may need to determine:

> Why can two computers connected to the same physical switch not communicate?

Instead of only asking:

> What does DNS do?

you may encounter:

> The workstation can ping `8.8.8.8` but can't reach a website by hostname. Where should you investigate?

This type of thinking helps prepare for scenario-based and performance-based exam questions.

# 🧠 Troubleshooting Rule

Throughout the projects, try to avoid random changes.

Use this mindset:

```text
Observe
   ↓
Gather Evidence
   ↓
Form a Theory
   ↓
Test the Theory
   ↓
Make a Change
   ↓
Test Again
   ↓
Verify
   ↓
Document
```

If you don't know why you're changing something, gather more information first.
