# 📝 Networking Fundamentals — Practice Exam 2

**Lessons Covered:** 11–20  
**Questions:** 40  
**Recommended Time:** 50 minutes

This practice exam covers the second half of the Networking Fundamentals course and reinforces concepts commonly encountered on the **CompTIA Network+ N10-009** exam.

---

# 📋 Instructions

- Choose the **BEST** answer for each question.
- Do not use the answer key until you finish.
- Pay close attention to **FIRST**, **BEST**, and **MOST LIKELY**.
- Use the symptoms provided in troubleshooting scenarios.
- Some questions may have multiple technically possible answers. Choose the one that best fits the situation.

Suggested study targets:

```text
36–40 = Excellent
32–35 = Strong
28–31 = Review weak areas
Below 28 = Review Lessons 11–20
```

> These ranges are study targets for this course and are not official CompTIA passing-score conversions.
