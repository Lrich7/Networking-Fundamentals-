# 🎓 Networking Fundamentals — Final Comprehensive Exam

**Lessons Covered:** 1–20  
**Questions:** 60  
**Recommended Time:** 75 minutes

This final exam covers the complete **Networking Fundamentals** course.

Unlike the earlier practice exams, topics are intentionally mixed together. Many questions require you to combine multiple concepts to determine the **BEST** answer.

---

# 📋 Instructions

- Choose the **BEST** answer for each question.
- Complete the exam before opening the answer key.
- Pay close attention to **FIRST**, **BEST**, and **MOST LIKELY**.
- Use scratch paper for subnetting questions.
- Troubleshoot from the symptoms provided.
- Avoid assuming facts that are not given.
- Try to complete the exam without using the course references.

Suggested study targets:

```text
54–60 = Excellent
48–53 = Strong
42–47 = Review weak areas
Below 42 = Additional review recommended
```

> These are course study targets and are not official CompTIA Network+ passing-score equivalents.
